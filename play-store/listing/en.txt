[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
LexGo brings legal services in Uzbekistan together in one app. Describe your problem and quickly find the right service and specialist.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• AI legal assistant — describe your question in plain words; the app identifies the type of problem and suggests a suitable service and lawyer.
• Directory of lawyers and legal advisers — search by region, specialization, rating and price, view profiles and place an order.
• Document preparation — fill in contracts, applications and other documents yourself, generate them with AI, or hand them to an on-duty lawyer.
• On-duty (urgent) lawyer — send a request for urgent help.
• Secure chat, audio and video calls — talk to a specialist inside the app.
• Orders and statuses — follow every step, download the result and rate the work.
• Plans — choose a package to extend AI and document features.
• Notifications — stay informed about orders, messages and calls.
• Help center — chat with a support operator.

FOR LAWYERS AND LEGAL ADVISERS

Register, publish your services, accept orders, work with clients and track your income.

SECURITY

• Mandatory two-step verification for staff and specialists.
• Password and biometric lock (fingerprint / Face ID).
• Data is encrypted in transit.

IMPORTANT

The AI assistant provides general information and does not replace advice from a lawyer. For complex matters, contact a qualified specialist.

Languages: Uzbek, Russian, English.
