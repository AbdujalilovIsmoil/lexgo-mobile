[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
LexGo brings legal services in Uzbekistan into one app. Describe your problem and the AI points the way, prepare a document in minutes, or connect with a verified lawyer.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• AI legal assistant — write your question in plain words. Answers cite the relevant articles of the law and suggest next steps.
• Ready-made documents — hundreds of contract, application and complaint templates. Search, fill in and download.
• Document analysis — upload a document and the AI flags risky clauses.
• Lawyers and legal advisers — filter by region, specialty, rating and price, view profiles and place an order.
• Urgent lawyer and SOS — request fast help in an emergency. Road-accident advice is included.
• Video consultation, chat and calls — reach a specialist securely inside the app.
• Orders — follow every stage, download the result and leave a review.
• Plans and packages — pick the one that suits you.
• Academy — legal knowledge and useful material.
• Help center — chat with an operator.

FOR LAWYERS AND LEGAL ADVISERS

Sign up, list your services, accept orders, work with clients and track your earnings.

SECURITY

• Data is encrypted in transit.
• Password and biometric lock (fingerprint / Face ID).
• Mandatory two-step verification for staff and specialists.

IMPORTANT

The AI assistant gives general information and does not replace a lawyer's advice. For complex matters, consult a qualified specialist.

Languages: Uzbek, Russian, English.
